What the page shows

The list is on the left and the map is on the right.

The Summary at the top of the list shows:

  • Number of parcels – how many land parcels your business has
  • Total area (ha) – the area of all your parcels added together, in hectares

Below that is a table of your parcels with each parcel’s ID, its main land cover and its area in hectares.

To sort the table, select a column heading: Parcel ID, Land cover or Area (ha). Select it again to reverse the order.

Find a land parcel

  1. Go to Find a land parcel.
  2. Enter all or part of a parcel ID, for example SX6795 or SX6795 2763. Spaces and capital letters do not matter. The table shows only the parcels that match as you type. If none match, you’ll see No parcels found.
  3. Select the parcel ID in the table to open its parcel details.

This only filters the table. The map still shows all your parcels.

To see all your parcels in the table again, delete what you entered.

Select a parcel on the map

Move the pointer over the map. The parcel under it turns light blue.

Select a parcel to highlight it. Its outline turns yellow and it appears above the table, with its row in the table also highlighted in yellow.

Select the parcel ID above the table to open its parcel details.

To clear your selection, select an area of the map with no parcel on it.

When the map is zoomed out

When you zoom out, nearby parcels are grouped into a blue circle. The number in the circle is how many parcels are in the group.

Select a circle to zoom in on those parcels. A circle showing 1 zooms to that parcel and selects it.

Map controls

The controls are at the edges of the map. Select Map controls in the top right corner of the map to show the buttons for moving and zooming.

Key to map controls
ControlNameWhat it does
Map controls Shows or hides the buttons for moving and zooming the map. They appear at the bottom of the map, with a target in the middle of the map.
Move up Moves the map up. The arrow buttons let you move the map without dragging it.
Move down Moves the map down.
Move left Moves the map left.
Move right Moves the map right.
Precision Turns small steps on or off. When it is on, the arrow buttons move the map a shorter distance (Nudge), so you can line the target up exactly.
Zoom in Shows a smaller area in more detail. You can also scroll up with your mouse, or pinch out on a touch screen.
Zoom out Shows a larger area in less detail. You can also scroll down with your mouse, or pinch in on a touch screen.
Map styles Changes the background map: Satellite (aerial photography, shown first), Outdoor (roads, paths and contours) or Light (a plain map that makes parcel outlines easier to see). You can also choose a Map size – Small, Medium or Large – to make the text on the map smaller or larger.
Drag the map You can also drag the map to move around it. With a keyboard, select the map and use the arrow keys.

What the map shows

Key to the map
SymbolNameWhat it means
Land parcel One of your land parcels. Every parcel registered to your business has a blue outline.
Parcel under the pointer The parcel your pointer is over. Select it to highlight it.
Selected parcel The parcel you selected. Its row in the table is also highlighted in yellow.
Group of parcels Shown when the map is zoomed out. The number is how many parcels are in the group. Select it to zoom in.

Using a mobile phone

On a mobile phone the list fills the screen and the map opens on its own.

Key to mobile controls
ControlNameWhat it does
Map view Map view Opens the map full screen. It is below the Summary.
Crosshair Marks the centre of the map. Move the map until the crosshair is over the parcel you want.
Select Select Selects the parcel under the crosshair.
Close map view Closes the map and takes you back to the list.
