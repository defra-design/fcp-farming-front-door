What the page shows

The parcel ID is in the heading, for example Parcel details: SX6795 2763.

  • Last updated – the last date a mapping change was recorded for the parcel
  • Total area (ha) – the area of the parcel, in hectares
  • Land covers – how many land covers the parcel has
  • Hedgerows – how many hedgerows the parcel has

The Land covers table lists each land cover on the parcel and its area in hectares.

The Hedgerows table lists each hedgerow by number and its length in metres. Hedgerows are numbered from longest to shortest, so if a hedgerow’s length changes, the numbers may change too.

Find a land cover or hedgerow on the map

Select a row in the Land covers table to highlight that land cover on the map with a yellow outline. The row turns yellow too.

It also works the other way round. Select a land cover on the map to highlight its row in the table.

Hedgerows work in the same way. Select a row in the Hedgerows table, or select a green hedgerow line on the map, to highlight it in both.

To clear your selection, select an area of the map outside the parcel’s land covers.

Look at another parcel

Your other parcels are shown on the map with a thin blue outline. Select one to show its details. The page stays open and the map moves to that parcel.

To go back to the list of all your parcels, select Back to land summary at the top of the page.

Measure a distance or area

Use Measure tools at the top of the map. You can have one measurement on the map at a time. Starting a new one removes the last one.

Measure a distance

  1. Select Measure tools, then Measure a distance.
  2. Select a point on the map to start the line.
  3. Select more points along the line. The distance updates as you go.
  4. Select Done when you have finished.

Measure an area

  1. Select Measure tools, then Measure an area.
  2. Select points on the map around the edge of the area you want to measure.
  3. Select Done when you have finished. The shape closes for you.

Reading the result

The result is shown in a panel on the map.

  • A distance is in metres (m).
  • An area is in hectares (ha), the same as the Land covers table. Underneath it is the area in square metres (m²) and the distance around the edge (the perimeter).

The figures are worked out from the points you placed, so they are a guide. They will not exactly match the areas and lengths in the tables.

Measurements are for information only. They are not saved to your parcel details or land summary.

Change or remove a measurement

Select Measure tools, then:

  • Edit measurement to move its points – drag a point to a new place, or select it and then Delete point
  • Clear measurement to remove it from the map

These are only available once you have a measurement on the map.

Key to measure tools
ControlNameWhat it does
Measure tools Opens the measure menu.
Measure a distance Draws a line and shows its length.
Measure an area Draws a shape and shows its area and perimeter.
Edit measurement Lets you move or delete the points of your measurement.
Clear measurement Removes your measurement from the map.
Undo While measuring, removes the last point you placed.
Done Done Finishes the line or shape and keeps the measurement on the map.

Map controls

The controls are at the edges of the map. Select Map controls in the top right corner of the map to show the buttons for moving and zooming.

Key to map controls
ControlNameWhat it does
Map controls Shows or hides the buttons for moving and zooming the map. They appear at the bottom of the map, with a target in the middle of the map.
Move up Moves the map up. The arrow buttons let you move the map without dragging it.
Move down Moves the map down.
Move left Moves the map left.
Move right Moves the map right.
Precision Turns small steps on or off. When it is on, the arrow buttons move the map a shorter distance (Nudge), so you can line the target up exactly.
Zoom in Shows a smaller area in more detail. You can also scroll up with your mouse, or pinch out on a touch screen.
Zoom out Shows a larger area in less detail. You can also scroll down with your mouse, or pinch in on a touch screen.
Map styles Changes the background map: Satellite (aerial photography, shown first), Outdoor (roads, paths and contours) or Light (a plain map that makes land covers easier to see). You can also choose a Map size – Small, Medium or Large – to make the text on the map smaller or larger.
Layers Turns things on the map on or off. Untick Land cover to hide all land covers, or untick a single land cover or Hedgerow.
Key Shows the colour of each land cover on this parcel, and the hedgerow line.
Help Opens this help. On a mobile phone it is under Map controls, and the map closes so the help can open.
Drag the map You can also drag the map to move around it. With a keyboard, select the map and use the arrow keys.

Land cover colours

Each land cover has its own colour. Some also have a pattern of lines or dots, so you can tell them apart without relying on colour.

Only the land covers on the parcel you are looking at are shown in the map’s Key. These are the most common ones.

  • Permanent Grassland
  • Temporary Grass
  • Arable Land
  • Land Lying Fallow
  • Permanent Crops
  • Woodland
  • Scrub - Ungrazeable
  • Farm Building
  • Farmyards
  • Hard Standings
  • Residential dwelling, House
  • Residential Gardens
  • Drain/Ditch/Dyke
  • Rivers and streams type 2
  • Rivers and streams type 3
  • Pond
  • Roads
  • Metalled track
  • Track - Natural Surface

Using a mobile phone

On a mobile phone the parcel details fill the screen and the map opens on its own.

Key to mobile controls
ControlNameWhat it does
Map view Map view Opens the map full screen. It is below the Summary.
Measure tools At the bottom right of the map on a phone.
Crosshair Marks the centre of the map. Move the map until the crosshair is where you want it.
Select Select Selects the land cover or hedgerow under the crosshair. If the crosshair is over another of your parcels, it shows that parcel’s details.
Add point Add point While measuring, adds a point where the crosshair is.
Close map view Closes the map and takes you back to the parcel details.

Print or save the map

Under Print map, click Print or save to open a copy of the parcel map and its details that you can print or save.
